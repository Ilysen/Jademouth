# This project has moved!
You can now find it on [Codeberg](https://codeberg.org/Ilysen/CoQ-Jademouth).

### Hey hold on, what's the big deal?
If you've followed my work before then this might come out of the blue! So here's some details about the whys.
<details><summary>Short version</summary>I have ethical objections to genAI and I do not want my projects to be on a site that continually pushes for its proliferation.</details>

<details><summary>Long version (i.e. old woman yells at cloud)</summary>
This might be the first time I've written a frustrated rantpost in quite a while. Hey, you clicked the dropdown, you have nobody to blame but yourself :P

My reasons for moving off GitHub boil down to this:
1. Generative AI is fundamentally built upon the industrial-scale theft, exploitation, and abuse of human creativity.
2. There is no ethical application of genAI, including at the level of an individual tool. This is because, as before, commercial models are constructed on mass theft. They cannot be divorced from this context because they would not exist without it, and small models running on one's rig also do not exist outside of this context unless they are built from the ground up to do so.
3. The advent of genAI has caused heavy and demonstrable harm to modding spaces. Single-use, unmaintanable vibe-coded software can be created and distributed far faster than hand-coded software could hope to match, meaning that by volume it has inundated mod lists everywhere and choked out the art of hobbyist creators.
4. GenAI carries a tremendous social and environmental cost. It funnels extraordinary amounts of wealth between the world's richest men in an incestuous cycle that produces enormous amounts of waste that is borne by all others.
5. GitHub has been at the forefront of all of this for years and shows no signs of slowing down.

Modding has become an increasingly upsetting activity in light of all of this. It is demoralizing to be unable to look through mod lists to see new things by cool people; it is demoralizing to have my spaces co-opted by folks whose modding career is measured in hours and who have neither any interest nor any intent of interacting with the artistic process in a meaningful way; it is demoralizing to be proud of my code work when so many view the act of creation as dumb labor to be automated away; it is demoralizing to be told that one of my primary creative outlets is not a legitimate form of expression; and, most saliently for here, it is demoralizing to push all of my work to a repository site that I don't even use most of the features of where I know it will be shoveled into the mouth of Copilot, against my will and against the terms of the license, so that it can be regurgitated on command with the digested remains of a thousand other projects and the resulting mess uploaded to the workshop or the Nexus or the mod list or whatever it happens to be for the game in question so that the person pretending to be a modder can farm clout.

I'm not under any illusions that migrating my stuff over to another hosting site will do anything to meaningfully stop the monolithic money-burning giants that are generative AI companies. I don't think that this magically makes my code immune to being scraped, either, as much as I wish it were true. But it does at least set me a little more at ease knowing that the code is on a website that is not so damn enthusiastic about it, and it makes me feel a little less like the one maniac who hates this crap when the whole rest of the world seems to have demonstrated a willingness to sacrifice any and all principles when given a shiny thing they like.

This shit sucks, man!
